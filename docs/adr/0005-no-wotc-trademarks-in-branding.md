# No Wizards of the Coast trademarks in the product name or branding

> **Deprecated 2026-11-27 by [ADR-0026](0026-personal-tool-scope.md): there is no product branding to protect. The `MinMax5e` name is kept anyway, since it costs nothing.**

The product is called **MinMax5e**. The name, logo, domain, and branding never use "D&D", "Dungeons & Dragons", or Wizards logos or trade dress. Descriptions may say "compatible with fifth edition" or "5E compatible", wording the SRD 5.2.1 legal text allows. The Fan Content Policy bars use of Wizards trademarks without consent, and renaming after launch costs search ranking, links, and recognition.

This rejects the spec's working title, "D&D 5e Build Solver & Optimizer".
