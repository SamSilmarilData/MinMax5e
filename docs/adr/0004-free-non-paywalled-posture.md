# Free to use, with no paywall and no required account

> **Deprecated 2026-11-27 by [ADR-0026](0026-personal-tool-scope.md): no product, no sales, no site. Revisit only if a public release is ever considered.**

Any non-SRD content (Great Weapon Master, Sharpshooter, most subclasses) can be used only under the Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy. The policy forbids requiring payment, a subscription, or email registration. So every feature and all content stays free, and the solver works without an account. Income, if any, comes only from optional donations, an ad-free supporter tier, or clearly labelled sponsorship, and the Fan Content Policy notice is shown site-wide.

This is hard to reverse: once users and contributors arrive on a free footing, charging later would need either a licence from Wizards or an SRD-only content rewrite.

## Considered Options

- Subscription or freemium access to the full 2024 content. Rejected: breaks the Fan Content Policy.
- A paid SRD-only product. Rejected: commercially legal, but it lacks the feats the product's core claim depends on.
- Two editions, a paid SRD-only one and a free fan one. Deferred: two content bases and confusing messaging. Revisit after the MVP.

Depends on the open legal question in ADR-0001.
